Welcome to Our Website

Eat Healthy, Live Healthy!

Healthy food habits help us stay active, strong and happy.

Learn about nutritious food and good eating habits through our website.

Healthy Foods

Fruits, vegetables, nuts, milk, eggs and whole grains are nutritious foods.

They help our body get essential nutrients.

Unhealthy Foods

Foods with excess sugar, salt and unhealthy fats should be eaten less often.

Examples include sugary drinks and some packaged snacks.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of foods.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Eat fruits and vegetables.
  • Maintain food hygiene.
  • Eat meals regularly.

Food Pyramid

Fruits and Vegetables: Provide vitamins and minerals.

Grains: Provide energy.

Protein Foods: Help the body grow and repair.

Dairy: Provides calcium and other nutrients.

Nutrition Facts

Carbohydrates: Provide energy.

Proteins: Help growth and repair.

Vitamins: Support various body functions.

Minerals: Help maintain health.

Water: Helps the body function properly.

Traditional Indian Foods

Idli: A traditional steamed food.

Dosa: A popular South Indian dish.

Dal and Rice: A common Indian meal.

Vegetable Curry: Includes different vegetables.

Millets: Nutritious traditional grains.

Food Waste Awareness

Plan meals carefully and serve appropriate portions.

Store food safely and use leftovers responsibly.

Avoid wasting food and respect the value of food.

About Our Project

This Food Habits project aims to create awareness about healthy eating and the importance of nutrition.

Through this website, we want to encourage people to learn about food and make informed food choices.

Conclusion
